HOW TO PLAY

The idea

You are back in Ward 7, at three in the morning, and the building is not empty. You need four things before the sun comes up: a way into Records, the code to Ward 7, a key from the nurse's station, and the gate key in the morgue.

The Matron only moves when you are not looking at her. Look away and she gets closer. Look at her and she is gone — but not gone for long.

Things to know

  • Your flashlight battery drains. It gets darker as it dies. Spare cells exist.
  • Look at documents. They are not decoration — the codes are in them.
  • Standing still in the dark is how the Matron catches up. Keep moving.
  • If she takes you, you wake up in Reception. You lose an item and half an hour.
  • Progress saves itself. If you close the tab, CONTINUE will be waiting.
